Potter’s Stages

Track every pot, every stage.

Pottery takes patience. A bowl might sit leather hard for a day, dry for a week, then wait on two firings before it’s done. Potter’s Stages shows you at a glance where everything stands, so nothing gets forgotten on the shelf.

Free, coming soon to iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Five stages, one look

Every piece sits in one of five stages. Touch and hold it to move it along, and the grid shows what’s drying, what’s waiting on the kiln and what’s ready to glaze.

  1. 1

    Idea

    Inspiration saved for later. Nothing made yet.

  2. 2

    Leather Hard

    Firm but still damp. Time to trim, carve and attach handles.

  3. 3

    Greenware

    Bone dry and fragile, waiting for the bisque firing.

  4. 4

    Bisqueware

    Fired once. Ready to glaze, or glazed and waiting on the glaze firing.

  5. 5

    Finished

    Glaze-fired and done.

Made for the studio shelf

Photos at every stage

Take a photo or pick one from your library and tag it with the stage. Each piece’s cover shows it as it is right now, and its history shows how it got there.

Save inspiration from anywhere

Share a photo or link from Safari, Photos or almost any app straight into Potter’s Stages to start a new idea or add to a piece you’re already working on.

Quick to keep up

Move a piece to the next stage with a touch and hold. Search titles and notes, merge two entries for the same pot, and flip between In Progress, Ideas and Done with one tap.

On all your devices

Your pieces sync through your own iCloud account to iPhone, iPad and Mac. Export a backup file whenever you like.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your pieces and photos stay in your own iCloud, and the app collects no data at all.
