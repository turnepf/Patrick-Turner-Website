Potter’s Stages Support

Help with the pottery tracking app for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Potter’s Stages follows each piece through Idea, Leather Hard, Greenware, Bisqueware and Finished, with photos at every stage.

Getting help

Email patrick@patrickturner.net with a short description of what happened and which device you’re using. I read every message.

Common questions

How do my pieces get to my other devices?
Through your own iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device with iCloud turned on, and give it a few minutes on Wi-Fi.

How can I tell everything has synced?
Tap the ⋯ menu, then Check iCloud Sync. It compares the pieces and photos in iCloud with the ones on the device you’re holding.

How do I move a piece to the next stage?
Touch and hold it in the grid, then choose Advance or Move To. Adding a photo tagged with a later stage moves it along too.

Can I keep my own copy?
Yes. ⋯ → Export Backup saves every piece and photo to one file. Import Backup brings it back, and skips pieces that are already there.

What about my privacy?
The app collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
